How a solar light works, in simple words

Think of it like a mobile phone. It charges in the day and gives light at night. Only here, the charger is the sun.

1. Sun shines

Even on a hazy day, the panel catches sunlight. India gets strong sun for most of the year.

2. Panel makes power

A high-efficiency panel turns sunlight into electricity. It is built into the pole or housing, so nothing sticks out.

3. Battery saves it

The power is stored in a long-life LiFePO4 battery. It can store enough for cloudy and rainy days.

4. Light at dusk

A sensor switches the light ON by itself in the evening, and OFF at sunrise. No one needs to touch a switch.

The technology, without the jargon

HPBC solar panel

A newer type of panel that makes more power from the same sunlight. That means smaller panels and faster charging.

LiFePO4 battery

Safer and longer-lasting than old lead-acid batteries. Rated for 3000 charge cycles, and no topping up of water or regular servicing.

Smart dimming

The light can run bright in the early night and soften later when fewer people are around. This saves battery for the next day.

Auto ON/OFF

Sensors switch the light on at dusk and off at dawn. Nobody has to climb a pole or press a switch.

Tough body

Rated IP65/IP66 against dust and heavy rain, and IK08 against knocks. Works from -15°C to +50°C, so from hill roads to hot summers.

Branded LED chips

Chips from CREE, OSRAM, NICHIA and others, with 150–170 lumens per watt. More light from less power.

Questions people ask

What happens in monsoon or cloudy weather?

The battery stores extra power, so lights keep going through cloudy days. Our HL Vion bar, for example, runs 3–5 rainy days on one full charge. We size the system for your city's sunlight.

Do I need to clean or service it?

Just wipe the panel with water now and then, especially in dusty areas. The LiFePO4 battery needs no topping up.

Can birds sit on the pole?

Yes, and that can leave droppings on panels. For HL Tejas we offer optional stainless-steel bird spikes.

Is it safe from theft and damage?

The panel and battery sit inside the pole or housing, so there are no loose panels or exposed cables to steal. The body is rated IK08 for impact.

Can I get a custom colour or height?

Yes. Most poles come in black or silver and many finishes can be customised. Share your site and we will suggest a height and power.
